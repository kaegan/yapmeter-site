He hears that you're talking, not what you're saying.

Yapmeter only checks whether there's speech-shaped sound, yours or theirs. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere. It only runs during a call.

One optional switch, off until you turn it on, adds Apple's on-device speech recognition to your microphone only, so the timer doesn't start when you're typing or when music is playing. It runs on your Mac, it keeps no text, and it is never pointed at the other people.

Does it record me?

No. It checks whether there's speech, then forgets. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere. Turning on the speech recognition switch keeps it that way: it runs on your Mac, on your microphone only, and keeps no text.

What languages does the speech recognition switch support?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Maithili, Malayalam, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu.

What about Teams, Discord, or Meet in Safari?

Not automatically yet. Pick Listen to All Audio from his menu and he'll listen to everything your Mac plays, for up to an hour or until you switch it off.

Does it know if I'm muted?

No. macOS doesn't tell apps that. If you're muted and talking anyway, the clock still runs.
