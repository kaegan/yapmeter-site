Does it record me?

No. It never stores or transcribes audio. It checks whether there's speech, then forgets.

Why System Audio Recording?

That's the only way macOS lets an app hear the other people on your call. It's only used while a meeting app has your mic open.

Does it know if I'm muted?

No. macOS doesn't tell apps that. If you're muted and talking anyway, the clock still runs.
